# RentaGO Technology Due Diligence Summary

## Ownership

RentaGO Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is the intended business and technology owner/controller of the RentaGO Platform.

## Technology

FastAPI, Jinja2, vanilla JavaScript, Oracle XE, background workers, Cloudflare Tunnel, provider-neutral maps, notifications, SLA/TAT/Policy, reporting, and backup tooling.

## Development

AI-assisted development was used with human requirements, review, testing, and repository control.

## Controls

Authentication, RBAC, tenant foundation, booking scopes, audit, backups, restore testing, CSRF, rate limiting, health checks, worker heartbeats, and encrypted backup controls are present at varying maturity levels.

## Outstanding Review

- Repository ownership is not verified in the current environment.
- Full tenant isolation and E2E/concurrency matrices remain pending.
- Provider delivery and webhook verification remain pending.
- Legal IP assignment, privacy, tax, transport, and SaaS terms require professional review.
