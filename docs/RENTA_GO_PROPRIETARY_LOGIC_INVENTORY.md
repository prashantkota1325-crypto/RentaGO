# RentaGO Proprietary Logic Inventory

The following are treated as RentaGO-specific proprietary implementation and confidential business logic:

- Corporate, Individual, Guest, Vendor, Driver, and RentaGO booking flows.
- Vendor/vehicle/driver allocation and compliance.
- Trip start/end, GPS trail, route planning, and deviation logic.
- Pricing, cancellation, refund, invoice, payment, and vendor billing workflows.
- SLA/TAT definitions, calendars, policies, rules, escalations, exceptions, approvals, and notifications.
- Feedback, safety incidents, CRM, reports, imports/exports, audit, tenant foundation, and white-label foundation.
- RentaGO UI/UX, database schema, migration strategy, deployment scripts, and operational runbooks.

Third-party dependencies are catalogued separately in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`.
