# RentaGO IP Ownership

## Business Owner

Legal/business owner: **RentaGO Technologies Pvt. Ltd.**

Product: **RentaGO Platform**

Business: technology-enabled car rental and mobility services.

## Proprietary RentaGO Materials

RentaGO treats its source code, application architecture, database design, migrations, booking/allocation/trip workflows, vendor/driver logic, pricing, margins, SLA/TAT/policies, notifications, reports, CRM, GPS implementation, UI/UX, APIs, deployment configuration, documentation, operational workflows, and confidential business data as proprietary materials.

## Separate Categories

- Open-source software: third-party libraries and components governed by their own licenses.
- Commercial services/APIs: Oracle XE, Cloudflare, Google Maps/provider services, SMTP, and future payment/messaging providers; service terms apply separately.
- AI-assisted development output: produced with AI tools under human requirements, review, testing, and repository control; AI tools are development tools, not owners.
- Developer/contractor contributions: ownership and assignment should be confirmed by written agreements with qualified counsel.

## Legal Review Required

An Indian IP/software lawyer should review copyright ownership, contractor assignment, confidentiality, open-source obligations, customer data rights, and SaaS agreements. This document does not make a legal conclusion or replace professional advice.
